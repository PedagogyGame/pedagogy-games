The Rehab Floor

You're running the gym today. Thirty terms, one after another, on the clock. Answer well and the floor stays healthy. Answer badly and patient well-being drops — and it does not come back on its own.

Well-being
Starts at 100. A wrong answer or a timeout costs 20, 25, or 30 points depending on how hard the term is. Reach zero and the day ends.
Clock
20, 25, or 30 seconds per question, set by how much there is to read and how hard the term is. Ordering and matching always get 30.
Bonus
Answer inside the first 10 seconds on a 20-second question, or 15 seconds on the longer ones, and you recover 10 points, up to a 150 cushion.
Formats
Mostly typing. Multiple choice, matching pairs, ordering, and true-or-false appear where the terms call for them.
After each
The correct term and its definition are always shown, including when the clock runs out.
