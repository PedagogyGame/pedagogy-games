Research library

The supplied research and recovered original documents are bundled with this release. The in-game Evidence panels distinguish source support from interpretation.

One Explorer, brought together

This is the full Unity Explorer game in your browser: 13 dated landscapes, 77 field stops, the atlas, people and chronology, direct feature inspection, and the field notebook.

Drag to pan; scroll to approach; shift-drag to orbit. Click the landscape, then use WASD to move, F for the next stop and N for your notebook.

Notebook saves stay in this browser. Export a notebook to keep a separate copy or merge it into another version. Existing notes are preserved on import.

Local reconstructions are explicitly dated references. The full 3200–500 BCE timeline does not imply continuous 3D coverage. Household layouts, vegetation and animal placement include interpretation.
