What one keystroke does
  1. Key pivotsThe front descends around the balance rail.
  2. Capstan liftsThe rear of the key raises the wippen assembly.
  3. Jack drivesThe jack and repetition lever accelerate the hammer.
  4. Escapement releasesThe jack slips away before impact so the hammer flies free.
  5. Hammer strikesFelt meets the string; vibration passes through the bridge.
  6. Damper returnsReleasing the key lowers the damper and stops the string.

Mode III removes cabinet and structural wood from view while preserving the parts needed to trace this motion.
