This is a complete playable HTML prototype of your editorial runway examination. The show maintains its own cadence, the models advance independently, and every question tests whether you can distinguish the right term from the nearest plausible alternative.

“The strongest vocabulary is not the most obscure. It is the vocabulary that allows you to say why an outfit works.”

Rules

  • Taste begins at 100. Incorrect answers deduct 20, 25, or 30 depending on tier.
  • Correct answers within the first 10 seconds restore +10 Taste if you are below 100.
  • The runway does not pause. Questions softly follow the most visible look about 60% of the time.
  • When Taste reaches zero, you are rejected instantly. If the show clears, you are admitted.

Controls

Click an answer, press 1–4, or use A–D. Use Enter to start and R to restart after an ending.
