About this model

This is a representative concrete gravity storage dam with an embedded vertical Francis turbine-generator. It is not a literal copy of one facility. Components that vary by project—surge chambers, fish passage, outlet works, and switchyard placement—are shown as auxiliary systems.

The drawing is a composite engineering cutaway. The generating-unit bay, spillway bay, outlet bay, and environmental passage would normally sit beside one another across the width of the dam; they are brought into one readable section here so their connections can be inspected together.

The live power estimate uses P = ρgQHη with 92% combined efficiency. The model distinguishes the turbine runner from the complete turbine, and the hydraulic passage from the dam’s internal access galleries.
