Welcome, Honored Guest

A doorman guards this restaurant, and he only lets in guests who know seven essential words. Once inside, join a delightfully indecisive couple at their big round table as they try to order one of everything — drinks included — entirely in Mandarin.

📖 Study Guide

The doorman only asks about one of 点 or 订 per visit — he alternates each time you practice, so both are worth learning.

Drinks usually arrive before the food — learn these and you can order the first round yourself.
