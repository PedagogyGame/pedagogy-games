60 DAYS UNTIL RIFLES — LAKE CASCADE

Terrain and environment
The core terrain, surrounding context and coarse skyline use resampled USGS
3D Elevation Program data. This is not surveyed terrain. Shoreline treatment,
the creek, soils, moisture, vegetation and habitat placement are representative
design layers. The game has no measured bathymetry or mapped botanical inventory.
https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services

Bird recordings
Credit: National Park Service Sound Gallery, public-domain recordings.
Sandhill crane: Denali National Park and Preserve, Alaska. Two edited excerpts.
Red-breasted nuthatch: Yosemite National Park, California. Three edited excerpts.
Excerpts were level-adjusted, faded and converted to mono. These recordings
were not made at Lake Cascade. No claim to original U.S. Government works.
https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-sandhill-crane.htm
https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-nuthatch.htm
https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm

Game rules
Tags, antler-point qualification, encounter probabilities and the bonus hunt
are fictional game mechanics. They are not legal Idaho hunting guidance.
Wildlife and plant models are simplified representative art.

Browser version
Unreal Engine's WebAssembly/WebGPU build runs locally in the visitor's browser.
The browser renderer differs from the native Mac renderer. Saved progress and
preferences belong to the current browser and website origin.
