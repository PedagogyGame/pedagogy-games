Vocabulary Study

A single-file educational activity. A definition appears, the learner types the correct term, easy items allow 15 seconds, hard items allow 20 seconds, and a correct answer inside the first 10 seconds adds 10 points.

Enter one item per line in either of these formats:

term | definition | easy

term | definition | hard

The session ends when all terms are completed, the score reaches zero, or all three lives are used.
