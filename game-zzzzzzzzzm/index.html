How to play, climbing & more controls ⌄

Climbing & movement

Hold W as you approach a tree trunk, or press E beside it to grab on. Use W/S to climb or descend. Select branches and wire junctions with A/D, then press E to transfer. Q drops you from an elevated route.

Stealth & survival

Hold the right mouse button to stalk, then left-click to pounce. Unaware people are easier to surprise. People may evade or defend themselves after an attack. P pauses the game.

Places & objectives

Find eight hidden chili peppers for temporary speed boosts. Bites earn Power and Squirrel Coins; Power decays after long stretches without a bite. Sycamore Park and Cedar Rest Cemetery are north; the hospital is west of the park, while older homes, estates, stores, and other locations spread across town. Some doors, garages, and vents use E.

Display & sound

M toggles the HUD, TAB cycles layouts, K chooses visible panels, and H toggles the control legend; C recenters the camera; L shows diagnostics. Use the VOICES button to mute or enable speech. ESC releases the mouse cursor.
