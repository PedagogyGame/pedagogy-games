RODENTS.

One very unusual squirrel. A whole town: sprawling tree routes, a commercial crossing, a wooded park, an old graveyard, a busy hospital and eight hidden peppers.

Run north to Sycamore Park and Cedar Rest Cemetery, go west of the park to find the hospital, enter the short sewer through E at a grate, or visit Chili’s and the gas station to the east. Find eight rare hidden peppers each run for a fierce, temporary speed rush. Successful bites build Power; every ten bite-free minutes drains 15% of it. Peppers never respawn during a run. Bites also earn Squirrel Coins for sewer outfits; staying motionless for thirteen minutes ends the run.
